I am Dani Klaassen, a self-taught artist from The Hague, Netherlands, born in late 2005. 


My work is driven by a deep passion for self-expression; I aim to reveal who I am through my art rather than explain it. 

I have always been fascinated by the human soul, which I define as the essence of beautiful people, and my lifelong goal is to cultivate that beauty in myself and others. 


I explore this through many

mediums, though sculpture holds a special place in my heart, even though I have produced more paintings to date.


For me, beautiful people are those who live fully, even in

struggle; who create, shape, and transform their own worlds;

who face pain and uncertainty with courage; and who bring

authenticity, love, and meaning into their lives and the lives of others. This is the life I strive to lead, and the ethos that guides my work.


I aspire to be recognized, to be seen as beautiful, and to create

works that hold meaning. I hope to build a life rooted in balance, peace, and love, to create a home, and to nurture children surrounded by care and beauty so they too can grow into

beautiful people.


Creating art is my path toward this vision, even when the way forward is not always clear.
